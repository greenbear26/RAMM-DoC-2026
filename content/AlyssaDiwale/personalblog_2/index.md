# Phase 2

Phase 2 has taught me so much about computer science and pushed me out of my comfort zone in many ways! Learning about  about MySQL, ER, and Relational Diagrams definiteley pushed me out of my comfort zone in many ways. However, it was all that much worth it seeing this learning apply through our models so far.

 Using my technical computer science side, I worked with Manav Mahida on drafting and updating models based on the data sets. For the models, I was the point person for the ER diagrams and helped draft the wireframes for each user persona. Specifically, I looked at our data sets and thought carefully about how entities relate to one another, and how this can merge in a global diagram. For the wireframes, I considered each of our three user personas — Tintin, Clouseau, and Stromae — and designed screens around what each user actually needs from the app, making sure the interface matched their goals and technical comfort level. This has been great excercise and I have learned so much already. I am even more excited to apply my learning in the upcoming Phase 3!

Outside of academics, I also thoroughly enjoyed the speakers in Brussels, visit to Atomium, and Ghent! The speakers, especially hearing the Lobbyist group was super insightful and I learned so much more about the EU during this talk. Outside of this, exploring more about Belgium like seeing the Manneken Pis and exploring boutiques in Gent has also been a highlight and I am looking forward to France and Luxembourg soon!
