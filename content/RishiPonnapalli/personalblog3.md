# Phase 3 blog
This phase, I contributed in 2 main ways. I first worked on creating our second ML model proof of concept. I found some new datasets, including the Popu-List dataset the speaker was talking about, and compiled them into a bigger set about European party ideaologies and locations. Then, I helped to train the model to predict the probability of a party being in the parliament or not. I also helped to put our first ML model in the streamlit site. I worked on setting up the model routes and structuring the model weights and data in the database, and then I wrote the frontend for the streamlit page that allowed users to use the model.

Outside of that, I've enjoyed the trips we had to Strasbourg, Luxembourg, and the chocolate making workshop in Brussels. All of these were fun, and I feel like I have a new understanding of the culture and history in these locations.
