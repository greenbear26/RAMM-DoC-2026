# Manav First Blog
Our team is developing a web application that allows users to query a policy area, such as artificial intelligence or climate change, and retrieve a comprehensive view of the surrounding lobbying landscape. The app surfaces data on organizational spending, industry affiliations, and country of origin, and includes a predictive component estimating each organization's likely political influence.

I led a portion of the dataset research effort, evaluating potential data sources for availability, usability, and coverage gaps. I also contributed to the development of one user persona, which shaped our design direction toward visual, accessible explainers rather than a dense research-oriented interface.

Maria Martin's guest lecture on EU decision-making was a genuine source of inspiration for our project concept. Outside of academics, the cohort trip to Brussels, including my first authentic Belgian waffle, and our first group dinner were memorable experiences that helped build a strong sense of community within the cohort.
